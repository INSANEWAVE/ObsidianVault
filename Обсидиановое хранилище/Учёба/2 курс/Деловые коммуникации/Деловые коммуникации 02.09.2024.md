## Introduction into Business communication

### Business communication as a practical appled science and academic discipline.

Communication is INTERCHANGING of an information.

Ways of communication:
1. Through the written word
2. Through the spoken word
3. Trough the pictures/diagrams etc. 
4. Through facial expressions, behavior etc.
5. Through non-verbal sounds

WHY is communication should be BUSINESS there?
That communication is supposed to convey an message, to achieve some points.

The MAIN FEATURES of BC
1. It deals with various commercial and industrial subjects
2. Characterized by certain formal elements (vocabularity, information)
3. Objective is to convey information accurately and concisely
4. Special oral techiques


### Communication functions

1. **Informative Function**
information is spread through:
Written communication.
Oral communication.
2. **Control function**
authority hierarchies.
formal guidelines.

Examples of control function:
	Written human resources policies and procedures
	Job descriptions - должностные инструкции
	Perfomance reviews

3. **Motivation function**
	Goals
	Progress
	Reinforcement
4. Emotional Expression Function
5. Integrative function
	Relates various components of organization and maintains equilibrium among them


### Types of business communication

Formal communication + Informal communication

Internal and External business activities.

Verbal and Non-verbal communication.

Based on the flow of direction 
	Horisontal
	Downward
	Upward
	Cross-channel
Based on the basis of responce
	One way - (advertisment)
	Two-Way - (manager discusses with the team)


### Principles of business communication
1. Accuracy
2. Brevity - краткость
3. Clarity - ясность
4. Courtesy


### Stylistic features of business speech

	Analytical - logic and thoroughness
	Functional - step-by-step instructions 
	Intuitive - big ideas rather than small details
	Personal - diplomacy and tact
